A quick check before you set off

Week 4, Wednesday · 6 pictures: swipe through

  1. Copy the words
    Before you set off, give your bike a quick check. Squeeze the tyres: they should feel firm. Squeeze both brakes: they should feel firm and stop well before the handlebar. Check the chain sits on the cogs, and fasten your helmet strap snugly. We check every bike before it goes out, but if anything feels wrong on your ride, stop and call Nigel on 07776 051559. Hills and brakes

Week 7, Friday · 6 pictures: swipe through

  1. Copy the words
    Hills are part of the fun round here, and a few simple habits make them easier. Change down to an easier gear before the hill, not halfway up it. Use both brakes gently, and slow down before bends. Look ahead, not at your front wheel. And if a bit is too steep, walking it is completely fine. Tell us who's riding and we'll suggest a route to suit. Save this for your ride.

Sharing the trail

Week 8, Monday · 7 pictures: swipe through

  1. Copy the words
    Sharing the trail keeps everyone happy. Ride on bridleways, forest tracks, cycle paths and roads, but never on footpaths. On bridleways, give way to walkers and horse riders. Slow right down for horses, pass wide and say hello. Coming up behind someone? Ring your bell or call out a friendly hello. And follow the Countryside Code: leave gates as you find them and take your litter home. Save this for your ride.
